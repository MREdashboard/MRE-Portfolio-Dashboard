Privacy Policy

Last updated:

MRE Portfolio Sync is a private, internal tool built for Marco Region Enterprises to consolidate financial reporting across its own group of businesses (Hand & Stone, Sweat440, Drybar, and BIMMI). It is not a public product, is not distributed to or used by any other individual or organization, and is not available for installation by the public.

What data this app accesses

This app connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's official API, using read-only access to Profit & Loss financial report data (income, expenses, and related accounting categories) for the connected company files. It does not access customer lists, employee records, banking credentials, or any data beyond standard accounting report figures.

How the data is used

Data pulled from QuickBooks Online is used solely to populate an internal financial dashboard for the business owner's own review. It is not sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party, and is not used for any purpose other than internal financial reporting.

Where the data is stored

Pulled financial data is stored only on the business owner's own computer and in a private data file used to render the internal dashboard. No data is shared with any external service beyond QuickBooks Online itself (the source) and the dashboard's hosting.
